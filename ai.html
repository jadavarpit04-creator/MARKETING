How a command becomes output

Two models, one promise: an answer you can paste — sorry, insert — without cleaning it up first.

Primary model answers first

Your context and instruction go to the primary model with the output format you asked for. Most commands end here, in a couple of seconds.

FIRST TO ANSWER

Backup model, automatically

Timeouts, busy signals and malformed replies trigger an instant handover to the backup model. The fallback is logged in your usage and never eats extra allowance.

AUTOMATIC FALLBACK

Validated before it lands

Output is checked against the requested format and the facts in your context — names, numbers and dates are preserved, never invented.

FACTS PRESERVED

Output in the shape the job needs

Ask in words; receive in structure. The same context can come back five different ways.

text
Clean prose for messages, emails and replies — ready to send as-is.
markdown
Headings, lists and checklists for docs, issues and notes.
table
Messy lists, expenses and metrics as aligned rows and columns.
csv / tsv
Spreadsheet-ready data you can paste straight into Sheets or Excel.
code
Snippets, regexes, SQL and type definitions — formatted for your editor.

Works inside your tools

No integrations to install per app. If it has a text field, Zerthik is already there.

Guardrails you can read

Sensitive apps stay dark

Password managers, banking apps and anything you blocklist are excluded by default. Zerthik cannot read what it cannot see.

Screenshots only on request

Screen context is captured per-command, shown as a chip, and removable. There is no always-on recording.

Every action is logged

Commands, fallbacks and insertions appear in your activity and usage — for personal accounts and team admins alike.

The latency & validation budget

Numbers we design around — and what “validated” actually checks before anything touches your document.

command card
Opens in under 300 ms
simple transform
Under 5 seconds end to end
screenshot context
Under 10 seconds, pixels to structured output
insertion
Under 500 ms into the focused field
format check
Output must match the requested type — text, Markdown, table, CSV/TSV or code — or the backup model retries.
fact check
Names, numbers, dates and amounts from your context are carried across exactly; gaps are flagged, not filled with inventions.
fallback
Timeouts and malformed replies hand over to the backup model automatically — still counted as one action, and logged.
final say
You preview before inserting. Edit, regenerate, or copy instead — nothing is sent or inserted behind your back.
