Pricing questions

How does the 14-day trial work?

Download the app and sign in — Pro switches on automatically for 14 days with the full allowance, MCP and API access, and no card is asked. If you do not upgrade, you move to the Free plan automatically and keep all your Shortcuts and settings.

What happens when I run out of actions?

Zerthik tells you before you hit the wall. Add the +500 actions add-on, upgrade, or wait for the monthly reset — your Shortcuts and settings stay put.

Do team plans pool actions?

Allowances are per user (2,500 on Team, 6,000 on Business), with minimum seats of 3 and 5. Admins see usage across the workspace.

Is MCP really included?

Yes — MCP connections are part of paid plans, not a separate meter. Server counts differ by plan; approvals and blocks apply everywhere.

Can I pay annually?

Yes — annual billing is roughly 20% less: Pro $96/yr, Team $154/yr per user, Business $269/yr per user.
